How do changes affect plants and
animals?
   A habitat is a living thing’s home.
Changes to a habitat can hurt the living
things there. Their water might dry up.
Food might become hard to find. Their
adaptations may not help them survive
big changes.

   Some living things may need to find a
new place to live. Others may need to find
new ways to live. Those that cannot move
or change their ways might die.
