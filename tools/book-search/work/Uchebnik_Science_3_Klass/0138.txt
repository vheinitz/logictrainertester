Building Blocks of Matter


What are elements?
   All matter is made up of elements. An element
is the building block of matter.

   People long ago thought there were four
elements. These were earth, wind, fire, and water.

   We now know that these were not real
elements. Tools have helped us observe over 100
elements. Some are listed below.
