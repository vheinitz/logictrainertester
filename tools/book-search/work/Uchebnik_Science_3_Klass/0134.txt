What happens when heat is
added to matter?
  Matter gains energy when it is heated.
This can change its state.

   If a solid is heated enough, it will melt.
This means the solid turns into a liquid. Ice
is water in its solid state. It does not take
much heat to melt it. Rocks can also melt.
It takes a lot of heat to melt them.
