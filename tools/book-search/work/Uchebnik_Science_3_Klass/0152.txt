Energy All Around


What is energy?
   Energy is the ability to do work. Energy
can make matter move, grow, or change.

  Energy can make things move. A soccer
player uses energy to kick a ball. Energy
moves from the player’s foot to the ball. This
energy makes the ball move to a new place.

                                              These penguins need
                                              energy to move.
