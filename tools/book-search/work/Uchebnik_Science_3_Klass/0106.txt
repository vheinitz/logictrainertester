What is a lunar eclipse?
  Once in a while the Moon may change
suddenly in the night sky. It may seem to
get darker as you watch. This is called a
lunar eclipse.

   An eclipse happens when Earth is between
the Sun and the Moon. This blocks the Sun’s
light. It can no longer reach the Moon.
