How does light travel?
   All light moves in a straight path. The light
from a lamp moves outward in a straight
path. Microwaves in an oven move in straight
lines. The light from the Sun moves through
space to Earth in a straight path.

   Light can hit objects. It may bounce off
them like a ball. Then it moves in a new
direction. It goes in a straight path again.
The bouncing of light waves off of an object
is called reflection.

  Smooth, shiny surfaces reflect a lot of light.
A mirror is an example.
