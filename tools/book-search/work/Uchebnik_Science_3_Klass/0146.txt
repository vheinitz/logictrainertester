What are chemical changes?
  Some changes cause matter to turn into
other kinds of matter. This is a chemical
change. The properties of the new matter
are different.

   A burning log goes through a chemical
change. The log starts as wood. Fire turns it
to ash. Some gas also forms. These kinds of
matter are different from wood. They have
different properties.
