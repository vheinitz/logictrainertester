A Our bodies are opaque and cast shadows.



  All opaque objects block light. They cast
shadows. These shadows form away from
the source of light. If you face a sunny
window, your shadow falls behind you.

   You cannot see things behind an opaque
object. The object blocks light from reaching
your eyes. A brick wall is an example of this.
Light from the other side cannot reach you.
