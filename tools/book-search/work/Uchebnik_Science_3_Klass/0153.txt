Energy can also make things change.
Energy in the air can cause a physical
change in ice. It can turn it from a solid to a
liquid. Energy from fire can burn wood. This
chemical change turns wood into smoke,
ash, and gas.

   Energy can also make things grow. Energy
helps plants make food and grow. All living
things need energy to survive.
