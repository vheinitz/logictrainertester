Freshwater environments have water
that has almost no salt. These include lakes,
ponds, and rivers.

  In saltwater environments, the water
contains salt. Oceans and seas are large
bodies of water with salt in them.

  Some special kinds of environments may
have a mix of both fresh and salt water.
