Lesson 2
Seeing Light and Color


How do you see?
  Light reflects off objects and goes into your
eye. You then see an image of the object.

   Some parts of our eye can open and close.
They can open to let in more light. This helps us
see when it is dark. They can close to let in less
light. This helps us see when it is very bright.
