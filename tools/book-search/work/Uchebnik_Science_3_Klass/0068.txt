How does a beaver change its
environment?
   Beavers make many changes to their
environment. They use their strong teeth
to cut down trees and plants. They build
dams across streams. This stops the water
from flowing and makes a pool or pond. The
beavers then build homes in the water.




                         Beavers use fallen trees, branches, and
                         mud to build their dams and homes.
