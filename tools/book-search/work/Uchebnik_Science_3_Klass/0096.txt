Lesson 2
The Seasons


Why do seasons change?
  Earth does not just rotate in space. It also
revolves around the Sun. This means it travels in a
big circular path around the Sun.

  The path Earth travels in is called its orbit. Earth
takes about 365 days to complete one orbit around
the Sun. That equals one year. The seasons change
as Earth travels in this orbit.
