What is light?
      Light is a form of energy. It is made of waves that
move up and down.

   You can see some light waves. We call these waves
visible light. You can see all the colors of the rainbow.

  You cannot see the other kinds of light waves. They
help us in other ways. Doctors use X-rays to check your
bones. Microwaves help us heat our food.
