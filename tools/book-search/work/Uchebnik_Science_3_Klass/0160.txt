Lesson 2
Using Energy


What is potential energy and
kinetic energy?
   A toy car at rest needs energy to move.
You can move it to the top of a hill. This
gives it more energy.

   That energy may not be used right away.
If the car is at rest, the energy is stored.
Stored energy is also called
potential energy.
   You can put the toy car on the hill and
let go. It will move down the hill. It uses its
stored energy to move. When a thing moves,
it has kinetic energy.
