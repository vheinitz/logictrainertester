Some dinosaurs of the past looked like
lizards today. They had similar body parts.
Maybe they used these body parts the same
way, too.

  Fossils can tell us if the animals ate the
same foods. They can also tell if the animals
moved the same way.




                 The dilophosaurus dinosaur (left)
                 looked like the frilled lizard of today.
