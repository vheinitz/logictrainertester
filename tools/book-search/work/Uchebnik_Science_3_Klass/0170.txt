How Sound Energy Moves




                                      This boy makes the
                                      guitar strings move. Then

How does sound energy move?
 Sound also moves in waves. These waves do not
move up and down. They move back and forth.

  Sound is a kind of mechanical energy.
Something must move to make sound.
You move a guitar string to make sound.
         Sound waves move through the air.
         Some of them enter your ear.

         Your ear has many small body parts.
         Different sound waves make them
         move in different ways.

         This lets you hear different things. Your
         ear tells your brain about the sounds.
