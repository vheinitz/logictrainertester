What are some sources of
stored energy?
      Fuel is something with stored chemical
energy. Wood, gas, batteries, and food are
examples. We can change the form of the
energy in these things.



  Stored Chemical Energy




       A battery stores chemical energy.

       This chemical energy can turn into
       electrical energy.

       The electrical energy turns into light
       energy in the bulb.
