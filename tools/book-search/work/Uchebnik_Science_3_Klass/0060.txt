What kinds of plants live in
wetlands?
   There are three main kinds
of wetlands.

     1. Marshes have grasses and
        reeds. There are no trees.

     2. Swamps have trees and shrubs.

     3. Bogs have moss and rich soil.
        Moss is a small, leafy plant.
