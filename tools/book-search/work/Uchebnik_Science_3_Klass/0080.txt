Lesson 3
Living Things of the Past


What can happen if the environment
suddenly changes?
  Thousands of years ago, ice covered much of
the United States. Then it grew warmer. The ice
melted and some plants and animals could not
survive in the warmer environment.

 They became extinct over time. Extinct
means there are no more left alive.




                      A This woolly mammoth became extinct when
                        its environment grew warmer.
