What are atoms?
  Elements are made up of tiny parts
called atoms. An atom is the smallest part
of an element that still has the properties
of that element. All atoms of an element
are the same.




  Atoms are very small. If Earth were the size of an apple,
  then an apple would be the size of an atom.
