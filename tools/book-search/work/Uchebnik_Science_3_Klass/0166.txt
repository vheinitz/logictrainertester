Lesson 3
Energy on the Move


How can energy move through
objects?
  Energy can move from one thing to
another. You can push a bowling ball
forward. Energy moves from your arm to
your hand to the ball. The ball may roll
and hit some pins. The pins may fall down.
Energy moves from the ball to the pins.
