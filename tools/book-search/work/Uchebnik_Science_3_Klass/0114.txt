What else is in our solar system?
   Asteroids (AS•tuh•roydz), comets
(KAH•muhts), and meteors (MEE•tee•uhrz)
also orbit the Sun. Asteroids are large pieces
of rock or metal. They may be pieces of a
broken planet.




              Asteroids may be pieces of

      Comets are chunks of ice mixed with rocks
and dust moving through space.
