During a dry spell, water in the savanna
might dry up. Some animals would need to
move to find new water. Others might dig
down into the ground and wait there for rain.

  Plants cannot move when their habitats
change. They might die. Animals such as fish
might also die if their habitat dries up.




 These elephants must move to ﬁnd food
 and water when the weather stays dry.
