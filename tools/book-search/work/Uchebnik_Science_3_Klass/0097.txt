Earth’s axis stays tilted as it revolves. This
tips the North Pole closer to the Sun in June.
More sunlight reaches that part of Earth.
Daylight lasts longer. Summer begins there.

   The North Pole tilts away from the
Sun later in the year. The sunlight is less
direct. Daylight lasts a shorter time. Winter
starts then.
