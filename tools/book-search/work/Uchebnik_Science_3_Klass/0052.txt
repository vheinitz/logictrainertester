How do plants survive in
the ocean?
  Most plants cannot survive in salt
water. Eelgrass can. It has adapted
to live in this environment.

   Most of what looks like ocean
plants are actually living things
called algae. Like plants, algae
needs water, sunlight, and carbon
dioxide gas to live. Some algae have
roots and can live on the ocean
floor. Other kinds have no roots and
