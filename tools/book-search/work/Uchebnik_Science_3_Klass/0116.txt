Telescopes: Discovering the Solar System


What is a telescope?
   Objects in space are far away. A telescope
is a tool that helps us see them. It makes far
objects appear bigger and nearer.

  Telescopes take in light with a lens. This piece
of clear material can change the path of light.
This helps us see distant things in more detail.
