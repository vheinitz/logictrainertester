Why does the Moon’s shape seem
to change?
  The Moon is shaped like a ball. It revolves
around Earth. This orbit makes its shape
appear to change.

  Half of the Moon is always lit by the Sun.
The other half is in darkness.

   The Moon takes about four weeks to
finish one orbit. It goes through all of
its phases in that time. This is called the
lunar cycle. The diagram shows how the
Moon’s shape appears to change over the
lunar cycle.
