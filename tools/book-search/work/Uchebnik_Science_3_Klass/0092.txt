What causes night and day?
   Our planet Earth is always moving. It
rotates, or turns. It takes 24 hours, or one
full day, to make one turn.

   As Earth rotates, one side faces the Sun.
It is daytime on that side. The other side
faces away from the Sun. It is nighttime
on that side.
