What are some forms of energy?
  There are many forms of energy. Some
are listed below.




                   Chemical energy can be stored in matter.
                   Gasoline and food both have chemical energy
                   in them.




                   Electrical energy can make machines work.
                   It gives machines the power they need
                   to do their jobs.




                   Heat energy can make cool things hotter.




                   Mechanical energy comes from things that move.
                   Wind has mechanical energy. We can turn the
                   wind’s energy into electrical energy.
