What are some other arctic animal
adaptations?
  Many arctic animals migrate each year.
They move from one place to another.
Caribou, tundra swans, and Canadian geese
are examples.

    These animals can not survive the bitter
cold winters. So they migrate south where
it is warmer, and where they can find food.

  The arctic tundra will get warmer in
spring. Plants will grow again. Animals that
migrated south will return.
