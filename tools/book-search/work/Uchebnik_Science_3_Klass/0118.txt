How did we learn about space?
  People long ago thought Earth was the
center of the solar system. They thought the
Sun traveled around Earth.

   Copernicus was a scientist. In 1543 he said
that the Sun was the center of the solar
system. Galileo was another scientist. In 1609
he used a telescope to show that Copernicus
was right.
