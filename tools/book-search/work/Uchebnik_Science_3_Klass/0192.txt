What are transparent and
translucent materials?
  Some objects let light energy pass
through them. This lets us see things on
the other side.

      Transparent objects allow most light
energy to pass through. Clear glass and
clean water are examples. You can see
things on the other side. The light energy
reflecting off them passes through the
transparent object. Your eyes can take in
this energy.
