Life in the Wetlands


What are wetlands?
     Wetlands are environments where water
covers the ground most of the time. The
water in wetlands can be salt water, fresh
water, or a mix of both. Most of them are
found where land and water meet.

  The ground in wetlands does not hold
much air. Plants need special adaptations to
get what they need to survive.




                                     The Florida Everglades are
                                     an example of wetlands.
