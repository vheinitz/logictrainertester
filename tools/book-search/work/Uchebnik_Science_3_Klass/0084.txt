How are living things today similar
to those that lived long ago?
   Some extinct animals are like animals
of today. Woolly mammoths were like
elephants. They were big and had tusks.
They lived in groups. They had flat teeth
and ate plants.

   Mammoths were much bigger than
elephants, though. Their tusks were much
longer. They had thick hair and lived in
cold habitats.
