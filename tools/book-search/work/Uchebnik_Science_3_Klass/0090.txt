Day and Night


How does the Sun’s position in the
sky seem to change?
  The Sun seems to rise in the east each
morning. It appears higher and higher in the
sky. At midday it is high overhead. Then it
appears to sink in the west. The Sun appears
to set in the west at the end of the day.
